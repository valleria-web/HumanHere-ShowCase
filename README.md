# Human Here

**Short live video moments.**

Human Here is a lightweight space for small live human experiences.

See something interesting. Join.
Have something to share. Go live.

Just people, present at the same time.

[Visit Human Here](https://www.humanhere.online)

## The idea

> I’m doing something in real life. Want to join me for a moment?

Trace a software system. Practice German. Cook something. Play violin.
Build LEGO. Share your screen. Talk.

A little time can be enough to learn something, make something, or enjoy
someone’s company. You can take part without building an audience first.

## How it feels

**Host:** Create → Publish → Check in → Enter video → Go Live → End.

**Participant:** Discover → Join now → Enter the room → Be there.

Leave when you need to. Rejoin while the session is live.

## Live means live

When Human Here says someone is live, a real human should actually be there.
The host enters the video room before opening the door to others.

Sessions are short by design. Human presence matters more than follower count.

## Presence, time and memory

**Presence:** Who is here now?

**Time:** Small moments, short by default.

**Memory:** What genuinely happened before?

## Current stage

Human Here is a working public MVP. Its validated flow includes discovery,
embedded video and audio, multiple participants, screen sharing, host and
participant re-entry, and ending a session together.

The current code also adds browser-reported presence counts, planned-time
presets, overtime status, optional banners, recently ended sessions and short
host summaries. These additions have automated coverage; their public rollout
and real-provider acceptance still need verification. Presence reports expire
when browsers stop checking in.

There are no accounts yet. This is an early space to try with other people,
share feedback, and help shape what comes next.
